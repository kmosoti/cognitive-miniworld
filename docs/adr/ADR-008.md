# ADR-008 — Baseline, ablation, oracle, and kill test required for promotion

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-08-27 · **Source:** EPIC §16

## Context

A primitive that improves an aggregate score may be improving it for
reasons unrelated to its hypothesis, or trading away safety. Without a
preregistered kill condition, a negative result becomes a prompt to
search for a friendlier metric.

## Decision

No primitive is promoted without: (1) a baseline it must beat on
identical paired seeds, (2) an ablation of itself from the full
composition, (3) an oracle upper bound where definable, and (4) a kill
condition declared before the benchmark runs. Each scenario freezes one
primary metric, its safety metrics, and a minimum meaningful effect
after the baseline pilot. Safety metrics are noninferiority gates.
Effect size and a paired bootstrap confidence interval are reported;
aggregate task reward alone never promotes a primitive.

## Consequences

Negative results are publishable outcomes, recorded in
`docs/verdicts/`. Exploratory metrics stay separate from confirmatory
ones. The benchmark default of 100 paired seeds is an engineering
policy to be raised when variance makes a conclusion unstable.
