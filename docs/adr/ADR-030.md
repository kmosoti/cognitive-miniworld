# ADR-030 — Replacing eligibility traces and delayed-credit gate

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Source:** MW-041

## Context

MW-041 must populate the eligibility field intentionally left empty by
MW-040, bridge a delayed typed outcome to earlier public contributors, and
retain global reinforcement as an executable ablation. The knowledge graph
requires explicit simulated-time decay, greater updates for true causal
contributors than distractors, and `credit-precision` guarded by
`viability-auc`. It does not authorize access to hidden world state or a claim
that temporal eligibility alone discovers causality.

## Decision

`CreditAssigner` is an immutable bounded replacing trace. A public
`EligibilityActivation` identifies a provenance-backed contributor event,
simulated tick, and strength. Reactivation replaces that contributor's prior
activation, with provenance that must identify the contributor. Assignment
multiplies strength by `0.5` once per elapsed tick and by the clipped absolute
magnitude of the public `ErrorBundle.outcome` teaching signal; a zero signal
returns the source trace unchanged. The decay uses a bounded plain-arithmetic
loop. At most 64 contributors and 10,000 simulated ticks are admitted. Ticks
are monotonic, contributors are canonical, and assignment requires the
supplied `ErrorBundle` to match the trace's error link. The output is a new
`ExperienceTrace` whose sorted eligibility entries, cost, provenance, and
confidence are recomputed. The source trace must be unassigned, and every
credited contributor must already occur in its public provenance.

`GlobalReinforcementBaseline` accepts the same trace, error, and canonical
contributor set but assigns the same teaching-signal magnitude to every
contributor. It therefore preserves the superstition ablation rather than
approximating it with a test constant.

The frozen `delayed_poison` gate uses seeds 1000–1099. The contributor IDs are
canonical public event-log identities rather than evaluator labels: the
executed tick-0 action and the simultaneous visible fruit cue activate at tick
0, and only the action is reactivated by its public tick-1 efference copy. A
paired consume-versus-wait training contrast derives the tick-6 signed
integrity teaching signal exclusively from public observations. Candidate
precision is causal weight divided by total assigned weight and must exceed
global precision by at least `0.15`; the minimum causal-to-distractor ratio must
be `2.0`. Both evaluator arms repeatedly select the contributor with less
assigned blame and share the same SHA-256 event-ID tie rule. Thus global ties
are neutral to action names and map to consume or wait across seeds. Candidate
`viability-auc` must be noninferior for every paired seed and on average.

## Consequences

The mechanism makes temporal eligibility inspectable, replayable, bounded,
and separable from the typed teaching signal. It does not infer hidden causal
structure: the confirmatory contrast supplies a public reactivation that only
the causal contributor receives. Credit weights support later learning, but
consolidation, policy learning, and habit invalidation remain MW-042 and
MW-043 scope. Changing decay, activation semantics, action adapter, seeds, or
thresholds requires a new decision record.
