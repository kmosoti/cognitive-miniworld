# MW-013 — Affordance generator

**Verdict:** accepted on 2026-08-28 in package 0.10.0 under ADR-023's frozen gate.

**Possible actions.** Positive joint belief support admitted candidates; explicitly false observable preconditions and impossible conjunctions were excluded without hidden-state access.
**Incomplete evidence.** Missing evidence stayed possible but unconfirmed, retaining at least two candidates in every incomplete factorial case with explicit lower/upper support bounds.
**Failure boundary.** The cycle observer distinguished no generated candidates `(true, false)` from candidates with no selection `(false, true)` without implementing MW-014 arbitration.
**Evidence.** Across seeds 1000–1099, feasible-best-action recall was `1.0` versus `0.5` for goal-only; invalid-action rate was `0.23076923076923075` versus `0.375` for enumerate-all, a `0.14423076923076922` reduction.

**Deviation:** none.
