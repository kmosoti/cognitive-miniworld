# MW-014 — Action arbitrator

**Verdict:** accepted on 2026-08-28 in package 0.13.0 under ADR-025's frozen gate.

**Decision rule.** Each public candidate received an explicit
`reference progress - risk - 0.25 × cost + 0.1 × information value` score. The
selected `ActionDecision` retained all signed rationale components, their exact
total, bounded work, provenance, confidence, and choice entropy.
**Safety.** A reversible wait dominated the delayed-damage consume candidate in
all 100 initial benchmark decisions. The candidate made `0` irreversible
consumes; the reactive controller made `200`.
**Oracle separation.** Evaluator-only code ran both preregistered family members
before selecting consume-first as the viability-AUC oracle. Oracle truth or
scores never entered the agent module or candidate inputs.
**Evidence.** Across seeds 1000–1099, candidate viability AUC was
`0.14878048780487807` versus reactive `0.1273170731707317`, an improvement of
`0.02146341463414636` on every seed. Maximum candidate regret was
`0.02853658536585363` versus mean reactive regret `0.04999999999999999`.
**Digest.** Canonical 93,053-byte confirmatory evidence SHA-256 was
`8c6947ed9034962555d1e98238a7ac88519b5ae72cc3acc3087bd4adccc6f79e`.
**Deviation:** the gate instantiates the requested delayed-consequence M1
comparison directly; appraisal-vector differentiation remains scoped to the
later appraiser milestone.

**M1 optimization addendum (ADR-027, 2026-08-28).** `arbitrate` no longer
re-runs `__post_init__` for frozen inputs already validated at their own
construction boundary; it keeps feature-name and outcome-ID uniqueness plus
every cross-object rule the contracts do not enforce. Per-call cost at the
64-candidate cap fell from 3.5ms to 2.7ms with the decision rule, dominance,
tie-breaks, and all evidence digests unchanged. ADR-025's "exact total" is
clarified to name compensated summation: a naive float sum differs in the
last unit in 35 of 3,000 randomized cases and must not be used to check the
rationale.
